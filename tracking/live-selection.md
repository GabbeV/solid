# Constructed live membership and landing selection

The two-action construction now supplies the actual live-list position needed
by the held landing scan. The seven new roots construct checked certificates;
callers no longer supply a lookup equation for this constructed family.

`live-membership.bend` represents an occurrence by an actual finite prefix and
tail with a list-shape equality. A present source's removal retains a distinct
target occurrence, including duplicate-containing lists. Presence matters:
the source's `splice(indexOf(id), 1)` behavior removes the final entry when the
source is absent. The proof follows the translated implementation and does not
replace that behavior with a totalized set operation.

`action-live-selection.bend` derives both occurrences from real allocation and
action opening, follows the actual join in either direction, and retains the
representative through arbitrary finite pair lookups/rejoins and either first
completion. Completion preserves the live list even for faulted helper inputs;
that frame alone is not a successful-execution or validity claim.

`constructed-landing-selection.bend` combines that occurrence with the existing
positive-debt blocker. Its `Selected` result contains a concrete index, the
actual lookup equality and the independently checked `Scan.Retained` certificate
for the exact core scan machine, World, caller stack, frame and continuation.
Initial World health remains explicit. It does not assume a chosen scan index.
The simple position getter is checked. An additional convenience function that
projected the full expanded scan equation exhausted the pinned kernel's fuel;
that function is excluded. The constructive certificate itself checks. No
runtime, payload domain, kernel or resource limit was changed.

`completion-live-history.bend` also proves that the actual certified rendered
completion/check audit preserves the complete live list over arbitrary finite
permitted histories. Initial occurrences therefore survive that audit.
`completion-history-landing.bend` combines the resulting occurrence with the
history certificate’s actual final pending snapshot and health, deriving the scan
position and exact held scan certificate on the actual final audit World. It
requires an initial occurrence, but no final lookup, count or blocker premise.

This closes live selection for the constructed pair’s first-completion prefix
and carries initial membership through certified rendered histories. It does
not establish general forest validity, unique live membership, arbitrary joins,
intervening writes/disposal, once-only delivery or execution of queued flush and
final coordinated reveal. Those remain separate obligations.
