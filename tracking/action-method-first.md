tail expression. Actual `Action.create`, opening, Promise construction and wait

registration lookup and the method equation are derived, not input premises.

wait append, observer allocation, wait marking, synchronous fulfilled/rejected

checked context contract preserves the action cells. Actual registration still
constructs a fresh unsettled entry after every such prefix. The actual

fulfillment or rejection of its retired wait retains the exact **current** Machine,

empty-prefix first-arrival family without changing those older domains.
